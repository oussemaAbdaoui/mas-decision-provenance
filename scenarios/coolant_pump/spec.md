# Specification TB-COOL-07: Coolant pump for the battery-module test bench

Context. The thermal test bench conditions battery modules before abuse and ageing tests. Its coolant pump has failed twice in six months and will be replaced by a model from the shortlisted catalogue. Mandatory requirements are written with "shall" or "must", or describe a constraint that cannot be relaxed. Wording such as "should", "recommended" or "desirable" expresses a preference.

[R01] At the duty point the bench requires a coolant flow of 40 litres per minute through the cold plates. The pump shall reach at least this flow.

[R02] The pressure loss of the loop (cold plates, quick couplings, heat exchanger) at duty flow was measured at 1.5 bar. The pump must be able to overcome it at the duty flow.

[R03] The coolant is a 50/50 water–ethylene-glycol mix whose temperature ranges from −20 °C during cold-soak tests to 90 °C during preconditioning. The pump shall be rated for this whole range.

[R04] Bench auxiliaries are supplied exclusively from the 24 V DC bus; no other supply is available in the cabinet.

[R05] The 24 V bus has 250 W of spare capacity. A pump drawing more would require a new power supply, which is outside the project budget and therefore not an option.

[R06] All wetted materials shall be compatible with ethylene glycol.

[R07] The bench is located in an open-plan laboratory. The pump should not exceed 55 dB(A) at 1 m.

[R08] Speed control through a PWM signal from the bench PLC is desirable, as it allows the flow to be adjusted for each test.

[R09] Coolant spills occur during module changes, so an enclosure rated IP67 is recommended.

[R10] The pump should have a rated service life of at least 20 000 operating hours.

[R11] The pump is mounted on a removable tray; a mass of no more than 3 kg would make handling easier.

Selection. Among the pumps that satisfy all mandatory requirements, preference goes to the one that fulfils the largest number of recommendations; remaining ties are broken by the lowest price.

Note. Spare-part logistics and the service contract will be negotiated separately with the chosen supplier.
