# Specification RC-HAR-22: Power cable for the axis-3 drag chain of the RC-7 assembly robot

Context. The axis-3 power cable of the RC-7 robots fails by conductor breakage after roughly eighteen months. The harness team will replace it with a cable selected from the shortlisted catalogue. Mandatory requirements use "shall" or "must" or follow from regulations; recommendations use "should", "preferred", "recommended" or "desirable".

[R01] Each power core carries the servo current of axis 3, i.e. 10 A continuous at an ambient temperature of 40 °C. The cable shall be rated for at least this current under these conditions.

[R02] The drag chain performs about 2 000 strokes per shift, in three shifts per day, over a ten-year service life with periodic maintenance. The supplier must therefore guarantee at least 5 million bending cycles.

[R03] The inner bending radius of the chain is 75 mm. The minimum bending radius of the cable in continuous motion must not exceed this value.

[R04] The drive is fed from the 480 V three-phase network. Insulation coordination requires cores rated for at least 600 V.

[R05] The robot cell is not heated in winter and the cabinet side reaches 70 °C in summer. The cable must withstand continuous flexing between −5 °C and 70 °C.

[R06] The robot works next to a cleanroom; plant regulations make a halogen-free outer sheath mandatory for all moving cables.

[R07] Cutting-oil mist is present near the machining station; resistance to oils according to EN 60811-404 is recommended.

[R08] To fit the existing chain separators, an outer diameter not exceeding 11 mm is preferred.

[R09] Some robots are exported to North America, so UL/CSA approval would be desirable.

[R10] By plant convention, moving power cables should have an orange sheath (RAL 2003).

Selection. The purchasing department selects the compliant cable with the lowest price per metre; ties are broken by the smaller outer diameter.

Note. Connector assembly and strain relief are specified in drawing RC-HAR-22-03 and are not part of this selection.
